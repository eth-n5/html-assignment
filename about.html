About the Department - Computer Science

History

The Department of Computer Science was established under the School of Mathematical and Computer Sciences to provide quality education in computing.

Vision

To be a leading department in Computer Science education, research and innovation.

Mission

To provide high-quality teaching and learning, conduct research, and engage with community through technology.

Location

University of Limpopo, Turfloop Campus, Mankweng, 0727. Mathematics Building, 2nd Floor.
